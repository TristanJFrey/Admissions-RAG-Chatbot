# 2. Undergraduate Admission

---

## 2.1 First-Year (Freshman) Admission

Application Platforms: Common Application; Coalition Application (where available)

Test Policy: ACT/SAT optional for Oxford through Spring 2026; superscoring considered if multiple test dates submitted

Application Fee: $50 (fee waiver available if selecting “Miami Fee Waiver” by Dec 1)

- Required / Supporting Materials

- High school transcript (unofficial accepted at time of application)

- At least one counselor or academic teacher recommendation

- English proficiency for non-native English speakers (international applicants)

#### Domestic First-Year Students

- Dec. 1 is the priority deadline for:

- Most of Miami’s merit scholarships.

- Competitive opportunities: Honors College, nursing, and majors within the Farmer School of Business.

- Dec. 1 is the firm deadline for:

- Presidential Fellows Program (PFP).

- Feb. 1 is the deadline for FAFSA.

- Deadlines & Decisions

- Early Decision (binding):

- Early Action I: Apply by Nov 1 → Decision by Dec 15

- Early Action II: Apply by Dec 1 → Decision by Feb 1

- Priority opportunities by Dec 1: Most merit scholarships, Honors College, Nursing, Farmer School of Business majors

- Presidential Fellows Program: Dec 1 (firm)

- Regular Decision: Apply by Feb 1 → Decision by Mar 15

- Spring Admission: Apply by Dec 1 → Rolling decisions

- Post-Admit / Placement

- Admitted students encouraged to submit ACT/SAT (if available) by early May for placement; not used to alter admission/aid after admit

- All new undergrads complete Miami’s math placement

## 2.2 Transfer Undergraduate Admission

Eligibility: Good academic standing; HS diploma required; 2.0+ college GPA generally expected (programs may vary)

Application Platforms: Common App (Transfer); Miami Transfer Application (no essay)

- Farmer School of Business (FSB) Direct Admission — Transfer

- 30 graded credit hours after high school

- Minimum 3.50 cumulative GPA

- Coursework: MTH 141/151 equivalent and ECO 201 equivalent

## 2.3 International Undergraduate Admission

Application Platforms: Common Application; Miami International Application (no essay)

Application Fee: $70 (may be waived via “Miami Fee Waiver” if selected by Dec 1)

Academic Records: Provide records from grade 9 to most current; final official transcript + proof of graduation required before enrolling

English Proficiency: Required unless exempt; accepted tests include TOEFL, IELTS, PTE, Duolingo; ACT/SAT may satisfy in some cases

# 3. Graduate Admission

Minimum Standard: Bachelor’s degree from an accredited institution; Graduate School admission required; programs may add requirements

Application Fees: Degree program: $50; Certificate/Non-degree: $20

- English Proficiency — Regular Admission Minimums

- TOEFL iBT: 80+

- IELTS: 6.5+

- PTE: 54+

- Duolingo English Test: 110+

- Conditional Admission (ACE-G) — Typical Ranges

- TOEFL iBT: 65–79

- IELTS: 5.5–6.0

- Duolingo: 90–109

- ELS: 112 certificate

- Process Notes

- Deadlines and supplemental materials vary by program; apply via Graduate School portal

- Some programs may require GRE, GMAT, portfolios, CV, recommendations, etc.

- Key Links (Graduate)

- Graduate Bulletin: https://bulletin.miamioh.edu/admission-graduate-students/
